The National Cadet Corps (NCC) is an important part of the co-curricular activities in our school. It provides students with an excellent platform to develop discipline, leadership, and a strong sense of responsibility. Our NCC cadets actively participate in various activities such as drills, parades, social service programmes, and adventure activities. They also attend different NCC camps such as Combined Annual Training Camp (CATC), Republic Day Camp (RDC), Annual Training Camp (ATC), Thal Sainik Camp (TSC), National Integration Camp (NIC), and Adventure Camps. These camps help students learn teamwork, leadership, and self-reliance. Through NCC activities, students develop confidence, physical fitness, and a strong spirit of patriotism and national pride. NCC in our school plays a significant role in shaping responsible and disciplined citizens of the nation.
